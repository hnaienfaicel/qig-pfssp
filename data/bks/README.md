Best-known solutions (makespan and schedule) for the Taillard and VRF benchmarks, version 10 (3 June 2026), from
O. Brunner, "Permutation Flow-shop: Taillard and VFR benchmarks best known solutions (updated according to linked
report)", Zenodo, https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.20529962, licence CC BY 4.0. Files are redistributed unchanged
(md5 checked against the Zenodo record). Columns: Name;n;m;LB;UB;Optimal;UBFoundBy;Permutation (1-based jobs).
Every schedule of the 160 instances used here was re-evaluated with qig.flowshop.makespan and matches column UB.
Instance names map as tai<n>_<m>_k -> Ta(offset + k + 1) with offsets 20_5:0, 20_10:10, 20_20:20, 50_5:30, 50_10:40,
50_20:50, 100_5:60, 100_10:70, 100_20:80; VRF names are identical.
